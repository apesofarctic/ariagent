# Ariagent — Business Model & Commercial Potential (Deliverable #4)

> The merge of Protect/Grow/Guide isn't just clean architecture — it's a complete P&L story. Each mode is a different revenue lever, and the shared engine is a platform that also answers problem statements #1 and #2.

---

## 1. Why a bank pays for this

Banks already spend heavily on digital channels but get shallow, reactive engagement. Ariagent converts engagement from a *cost center* (notifications nobody reads) into a *revenue and retention engine* — while improving customer trust, the hardest thing to buy.

The core commercial claim: **a proactive companion that acts first monetises moments the bank currently misses entirely** — the overdraft caught, the surplus invested, the life event served.

---

## 2. Revenue map (each mode → a lever)

| Mode | Customer value | Bank revenue lever | Example products |
|---|---|---|---|
| 🛡 **Protect** | Avoids overdrafts, fraud loss, wasted subscriptions | **Retention + NII + reduced servicing cost** | Buffer credit / overdraft lines, fraud-protect tier, fewer NSF complaints → lower churn & call-center load |
| 📈 **Grow** | Money put to work automatically | **AUM + fee income + deposit stickiness** | SIPs, mutual funds, high-yield sweeps, recurring deposits |
| 🧭 **Guide** | Supported through life's big moments | **Cross-sell bundles (highest revenue/event)** | Life/health insurance, goal savings, home/auto loans, child plans |

**Key point:** GUIDE generates the highest revenue-per-interaction because one life event bundles *multiple* products (insurance + investment + savings) sold at the moment of genuine need, not via cold push.

## 3. The platform multiplier (answers all three problem statements)

The same Sense→Reason→Decide→Act→Explain engine generalises beyond engagement:

- **#3 Digital Engagement** — the core product (Protect/Grow/Guide).
- **#1 Customer Acquisition** — point the detectors at prospects/onboarding signals → conversational onboarding + next-best-action for relationship managers.
- **#2 Digital Adoption** — point them at feature-usage signals → an adaptive in-app coach driving payments/investments/insurance adoption.

So the investment is **one architecture, three problem statements** — a platform, not a feature. This materially raises the ceiling on commercial value and is a strong differentiator for judges.

---

## 4. Commercial / pricing models (how it's sold)

1. **B2B2C SaaS to banks** — per-active-user/month platform fee. Predictable, scales with the bank's base.
2. **Outcome / revenue-share** — a share of incremental product revenue and AUM attributable to Ariagent-driven actions (instrumentable via the audit log — every action is attributable).
3. **Cost-avoidance value-based** — priced against measurable reductions in fraud loss, NSF/overdraft complaints, and servicing cost.
4. **Tiered autonomy upsell** — Suggest (base) → Standing-rules → **Pulse autopilot** (premium), each tier a higher price point as trust and delegation deepen.

Most credible go-to-market: land on **B2B2C SaaS + cost-avoidance** (easy to underwrite), expand to **revenue-share** once attribution is proven.

---

## 5. Unit economics narrative (illustrative)

For a mid-size retail bank with **5M digital customers**:
- North star: **proactive actions accepted / active user / month.** Even a modest **2–3 accepted actions/user/month** at single-digit-% conversion to a revenue product compounds across millions of users.
- **Protect** reduces churn and servicing cost (each avoided overdraft complaint saves call-center cost; each retained customer preserves lifetime value).
- **Grow + Guide** lift cross-sell ratio (products-per-customer) — the single biggest driver of retail-bank profitability.

The pitch number to feature in the deck: pick **products-per-customer uplift** and **AUM activated from idle balances** as the two headline outcomes — both directly attributable and CFO-legible.

## 6. Metric tree (north star → drivers → guardrail)

```
NORTH STAR:  Proactive Actions Accepted / Active User / Month
   │
   ├── PROTECT drivers:  overdrafts avoided · fraud caught · ₹ saved · churn ↓
   ├── GROW drivers:     surplus activated (₹ moved to productive) · SIPs started · AUM ↑
   └── GUIDE drivers:    journeys started → completed · products per journey · cross-sell ratio ↑
   
GUARDRAIL METRIC:  nudge acceptance rate & opt-out rate
   (proves the system is helpful, not spammy — directly ties to the Orchestrator's restraint)
```

The guardrail metric matters as much as the north star: it's the proof that proactivity didn't become spam. A rising acceptance rate with a falling opt-out rate is the evidence the Orchestrator's judgment works.

---

## 7. Moat / defensibility

- **Trust + reasoning transparency** — showing the "why" on every action builds the trust competitors' black-box nudges can't, and trust compounds.
- **Data + memory flywheel** — the longer Ariagent serves a customer, the better its financial twin and the more accurate its judgment; switching cost rises.
- **Staged-autonomy lock-in** — once a customer grants Tier-2/Tier-3 delegation, Ariagent becomes infrastructure for their financial life, not an app they can casually swap.
- **Compliance-by-design** — the guardrail/audit layer is a barrier to fast-follow entrants in a regulated market.
